% Recap of the day. Three to five points, then the one point to keep.
\begin{frame}{Recap}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Latency, bandwidth, privacy, cost, and availability move inference
          to the edge. The four paradigms are cloud, edge, mobile, and
          TinyML.
    \item A model has three budgets: parameters, operations, and activation
          memory. A model can meet one budget and fail a different one.
    \item Depthwise separable convolutions and inverted residuals decrease
          the cost of a layer.
    \item The edge AI workflow has six stages: data, training, optimization,
          conversion, deployment, and monitoring.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.5em
  \keypoint{Compare the flash and the RAM of the device with the size and
            the peak activation memory of the model before you deploy.}
\end{frame}
