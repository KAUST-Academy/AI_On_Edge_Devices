\begin{frame}{If a part does not work, and common problems}
  \footnotesize
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
  \setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
  \begingroup\centering
  \begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.40\textwidth}L{0.55\textwidth}@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Problem} & \textbf{Fix or fallback} \\
    \midrule
    The download of the keyword dataset fails
      & The instructor gives the file \code{keywords2.zip}. Put it into
        \code{downloads/} and run the script again. \\
    The Studio is not available, or the training is not complete in time
      & Use the library ZIP file that the instructor gives. Write this in
        the report. \\
    The build of the library fails with the core 3.3.12
      & Install the core 2.0.17 and build again. The two sketches work
        with the two cores. \\
    The build prints \code{...\_inferencing.h: No such file}
      & Change the first \code{\#include} line to the header of your
        library. \\
    \code{not enough memory}, or \code{Camera init failed}
      & Set \code{Tools > PSRAM > OPI PSRAM}. \\
    One word gives two or three events
      & Complete task A2, or increase \code{SUPPRESSION\_MS}. \\
    A spoken word gives no event
      & Use fewer windows, a lower threshold, or a smaller stride. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}\par\endgroup
  \vskip 0.4em
  \small
  The README of the lab has the complete list.
\end{frame}
