% Goal and plan of the Day 5 lab.
% The parts and the times come from the Day 5 section of the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} your group has one audio model and one vision model
          on the XIAOML Kit: a keyword spotter that makes one event for
          each spoken keyword, and an image classifier that shows the class
          on the display.
    \item \textbf{Board:} the XIAOML Kit, with the microphone, the camera,
          and the display.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} two live demonstrations, and the file
          \code{report.md} with the measurement table and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the features, the two models, and the
    post-processing. In the lab, Edge Impulse Studio makes the features and
    the models. You write the post-processing, you tune it, and you measure
    the two models on the board.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{The model comes from a tool in some minutes. The work of the
    engineer is the data before the model and the decisions after it.}
\end{frame}
