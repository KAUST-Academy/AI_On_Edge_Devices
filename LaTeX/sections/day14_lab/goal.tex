% Goal and plan of the Day 14 lab.

\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} an approved capstone proposal and a started build.
    \item \textbf{Boards:} the XIAOML Kit and the Raspberry Pi 5 of both
          lab groups of your team.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} the approved proposal \code{proposal.md},
          and the file \code{report.md} with the budgets, the result of the
          review, the result of the first test, and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    Part 3 of the lecture gave a design method in seven steps, the
    budgets for latency, power, and cost, and the questions of a design
    review. Today your team applies them to its own system.
    \code{Docs/capstone.md} is the capstone brief.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{Test the riskiest assumption today. A plan that fails on
    Day 14 can still change.}
\end{frame}
