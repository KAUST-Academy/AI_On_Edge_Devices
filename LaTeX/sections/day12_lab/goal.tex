% Goal and plan of the Day 12 lab.

\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} a system of two boards that decides locally and
          sends its results to the cloud, also when the link to the cloud
          fails.
    \item \textbf{Boards:} the XIAOML Kit and the Raspberry Pi 5. The
          instructor laptop is the ``cloud''.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} a live demonstration with the link to the
          cloud connected and cut, and the file \code{report.md} with the
          topic tree, the measured values, and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the decision layer with a state machine, the MQTT
    topics, the QoS levels, the last will, and store and forward. Its values
    came from one computer with two brokers. Today the messages cross the
    Wi-Fi, the keyword model of Day 5 makes the events, and you cut the link
    yourself.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{The local action must work with no cloud, and no message may be
    lost when the cloud comes back.}
\end{frame}
