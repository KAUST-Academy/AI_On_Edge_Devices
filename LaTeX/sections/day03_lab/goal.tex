% Goal and plan of the Day 3 lab.
% The parts and the times come from the Day 3 section of the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} your group has a motion classifier that runs on the
          XIAOML Kit, and you know its flash use, its arena size, and its
          latency.
    \item \textbf{Board:} the XIAOML Kit, with the Arduino firmware.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} a live demonstration of the four motions,
          and the file \code{report.md} with the comparison table of the two
          paths and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the format, the runtime, and the features. In the lab,
    you train a model on your Day 2 dataset, you convert it, you write the
    inference code, and you measure the three numbers on the board.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{A number from the laptop is an estimate. Only the number from
    the board is a result.}
\end{frame}
