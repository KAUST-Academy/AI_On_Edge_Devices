% Goal and plan of the Day 6 lab.

\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} your group has a trade-off curve for one task, and
          a model selection for the two boards of the course.
    \item \textbf{Boards:} none. The lab runs on the laptop.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} the plot \code{tradeoff.png} with at least
          six models, and the file \code{report.md} with your selection
          and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave three ways to a smaller model and a method for the
    selection. In the lab, you prune a CNN, you distill a teacher into a
    student, you quantize the candidates, and you select one model for
    each board from your own plot.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{A method is good only for a given budget. The plot shows all
    candidates. The budget selects one of them.}
\end{frame}
