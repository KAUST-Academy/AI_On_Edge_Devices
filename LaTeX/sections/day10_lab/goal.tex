% Goal and plan of the Day 10 lab.
% The parts and the times come from the Day 10 section of the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} a measured comparison of two small language models
          on the Raspberry Pi 5, and one working application.
    \item \textbf{Board:} the Raspberry Pi 5 (8 GB) with the active cooler.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} the file \code{report.md} with the benchmark
          table, the results of the application, and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the RAM budget, the two rates, the options of Ollama,
    and the methods that connect a model to a program. Its rates came from
    the sources and from an x86 computer. Today you measure the board, and
    you check the output of each model with a schema and with your code.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{A rate needs its method. An output needs a check: valid is not
    the same as correct.}
\end{frame}
