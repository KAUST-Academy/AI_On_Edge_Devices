% Goal and plan of the Day 11 lab.

\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} the Day 8 detector runs on a live network video
          stream, and you know the age of the image that it sees.
    \item \textbf{Boards:} the Raspberry Pi 5 with its camera, and the
          XIAOML Kit with its camera.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} a live demonstration, and the file
          \code{report.md} with the network values, the latency for two
          stream settings, the values of the XIAO stream, and the Decision
          Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the network tools, the bit rates of MJPEG and H.264,
    RTSP, and the buffers that make a frame old. Its values came from one
    computer with no network and no camera. Today you measure the lab
    network, the two cameras, and the complete path from the camera to the
    boxes on the screen.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{Measure the age of the image at the moment of the result, not
    only the time of the model.}
\end{frame}
