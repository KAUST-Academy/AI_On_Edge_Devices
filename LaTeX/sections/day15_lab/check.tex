\begin{frame}{Check criterion}
  \small
  The instructor grades with the capstone rubric (\code{Docs/capstone.md},
  Section 3):
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \cmark\ The system works in the demonstration (30 percent).
    \item \cmark\ Integration of the two boards, messages, and monitoring
          (25 percent).
    \item \cmark\ Measured results and analysis of trade-offs (25 percent).
    \item \cmark\ Report and presentation (20 percent).
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \textbf{Hand in:} \code{report.md}, and \code{feedback.md} with no name.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Decision Log}
  \begin{exerciseblock}
    Which decision of your system had the largest effect on the result,
    and which measured number shows it?
  \end{exerciseblock}
  \vskip 0.2em
  The report has two or three Decision Logs of about 100 words each. A
  good one:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item states the decision and the options,
    \item gives at least two measured numbers with their method,
    \item names the trade-off: what you gained and what you gave up,
    \item says which numbers are estimates.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
