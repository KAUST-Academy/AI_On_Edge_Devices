% Goal and plan of the Day 15 lab.
% The parts and the times come from the Day 15 section of the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} a working capstone system and a short report.
    \item \textbf{Boards:} the XIAOML Kits and the Raspberry Pi 5 boards of
          your team.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} the demonstration of 10 minutes and the
          report \code{report.md}.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{The last day}
    After Part 1 of the lecture, the lecture time (Parts 2 and 3, 100
    minutes) is build time. The instructor visits each team. Then the lab
    has 80 minutes of build and measurement, the demonstrations, and the
    close. \code{Docs/capstone.md} gives the requirements and the rubric.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{Finish the measurements before the polish. A working system
    with numbers scores more than a nice system with no numbers.}
\end{frame}
