% Recap of the day. Three to five points, then the one point to keep.
\begin{frame}{Recap}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item TCP gives a reliable, ordered byte stream and resends lost data. UDP
          sends single datagrams with no resend. Video and time data often use
          UDP, because an old packet has no value.
    \item Bandwidth, latency, and jitter are three different numbers. Measure
          them with iperf3 and ping, and find the addresses and the open ports
          with ip and ss.
    \item An H.264 stream sends a keyframe and then only the changes, so it
          needs much less bandwidth than MJPEG. RTSP controls the session, RTP
          carries the video, and RTCP reports the quality.
    \item The end-to-end latency is the sum of capture, encoding, network,
          buffers, decoding, and inference. A reader that keeps only the
          newest image gives a low latency and drops old images.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.5em
  \keypoint{Measure the age of the image that the model sees, not only the
            latency of the model. Select the place of the inference from the
            bandwidth, the latency, and the privacy of the video.}
\end{frame}
