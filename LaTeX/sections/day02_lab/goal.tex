% Goal and plan of the Day 2 lab.
% The parts and the times come from the Day 2 section of the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} your group has a labelled motion dataset that
          Day 3 uses, with a constant sampling rate and a correct split
          between training and test data.
    \item \textbf{Board:} the XIAOML Kit.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} the folder \code{data/} with your recordings
          and the file \code{split.json}, and the file \code{report.md} with
          the data card and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the rules: a timer for a constant rate, a window that
    holds the pattern, and a split by session. In the lab, you measure your
    rate, you record four motion classes, and you measure the effect of a
    wrong split.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{Day 3 trains a model on the dataset of today. An error in the
    data today gives a wrong accuracy tomorrow.}
\end{frame}
