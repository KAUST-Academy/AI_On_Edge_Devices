% Goal and plan of the Day 1 lab.
% The parts and the times come from the Day 1 section of the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} your group has a toolchain that works, and a first
          resource budget for each device class.
    \item \textbf{Board:} the XIAOML Kit. Each group has two students and one
          kit.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} the file \code{report.md} with the results of
          the five sensor tests, the completed table ``model and device'',
          and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the method: three budgets of a model, and two budgets of
    a device. In the lab, you read the budgets of your board, and you
    calculate the budgets of three models.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{You decide from flash and RAM numbers if a model fits a device.}
\end{frame}
