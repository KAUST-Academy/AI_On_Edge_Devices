    \midrule
    The Arduino IDE shows no port
      & Use a data cable. On Linux, do step 9 of \code{Labs/SETUP.md}. \\
    The upload fails with a connection error
      & Disconnect the cable. Hold the \code{B} (boot) button of the XIAO.
        Connect the cable, then release the button. Upload again. \\
    The Serial Monitor shows nothing or wrong characters
      & Set 115200 baud. \\
    \code{imu\_test} prints \code{IMU initialization failed}
      & Press the XIAO on the expansion board until all pins are in. \\
    \code{mic\_test} prints nothing
      & Open the Serial Plotter or the Serial Monitor. The sketch waits for
        it. \\
    \code{camera\_test} prints \code{PSRAM is not active}
      & Set \code{Tools > PSRAM > OPI PSRAM} and upload
        again. \\
    \code{WiFiScan} finds no network
      & Connect the antenna to the small connector on the XIAO. \\
    The notebook prints \code{No module named 'torch'}
      & Start Jupyter with \code{.venv/bin/jupyter lab}. \\
    \bottomrule
