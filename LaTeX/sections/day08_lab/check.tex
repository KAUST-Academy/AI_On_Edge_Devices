\begin{frame}{Decision Log}
  Write about 100 words. State one design decision, give your measured
  numbers, and name the trade-off.
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{exerciseblock}
    A product with a Raspberry Pi 5 and a camera must count the bottles
    and the cups on a table, with 10 frames in each second or more.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Which file do you select: which runtime, which image size, and
            which precision?
    \end{itemize}
  \end{exerciseblock}
  \vskip 0.2em
  A good Decision Log:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item gives the frame rate and the mAP50-95 of your selection and of
          one other file from your table,
    \item compares the frame rate with the target of 10 frames in each
          second,
    \item names what you lose with your decision. Example: the file for
          320 pixels is faster, and it finds fewer small objects.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
