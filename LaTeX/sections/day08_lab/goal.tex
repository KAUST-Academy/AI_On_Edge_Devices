% Goal and plan of the Day 8 lab.
% The parts and the times come from the Day 8 section of the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} your group has a live detection on the Raspberry
          Pi 5, with a custom model for two classes: a bottle and a cup.
    \item \textbf{Board:} the Raspberry Pi 5 with the camera.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} a live demonstration, and the frame-rate
          table and the Decision Log in the file \code{report.md}.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the method: the candidates of a detector, the
    suppression, the IoU, the mAP, and the four decisions of a deployment.
    All its times are from an x86 processor. In the lab, you train a
    detector, you export it, and you measure its frame rate and its
    accuracy for the board.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{A frame rate has no value without the accuracy of the same
    file. The table of this lab has the two numbers in each row.}
\end{frame}
