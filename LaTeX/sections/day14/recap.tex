% Recap of the day. Three to five points, then the one point to keep.
\begin{frame}{Recap}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Each device knows the version of its model and its program. An
          update goes to a few devices first, then to more. A device keeps the
          old version and returns to it when the new one fails.
    \item The attack surface of an edge device includes its network, its
          storage, and its physical access. Encrypted transport, a key for
          each device, signed updates, and data minimization reduce the risks.
    \item A watchdog restarts a stopped program. A device with a failed part
          goes to a safe, reduced operation and reports it.
    \item A design starts from the requirements: the latency, the energy, the
          privacy, and the cost. They select the paradigm, the boards, the
          model, and the pipeline. A review finds the weak points before the
          build.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.5em
  \keypoint{Plan the updates, the threats, and the budgets before the build,
            because a deployed device is hard to change.}
\end{frame}
