% Motivation and learning outcomes.
% The outcomes are the outcomes of Day 14 in the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Why this day matters}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item On Days 1 to 13 you built the parts of an edge AI system: a model on
          a board, a stream, messages, decisions, and monitoring. A product
          has many devices that run for years.
    \item Each device needs new models and fixes over a bad network. A failed
          update must not stop the device. The devices of one fleet often have
          different hardware.
    \item A device in a public place can be stolen, opened, or attacked
          through its network. Its model, its keys, and its data need
          protection.
    \item Today you also start the capstone project: your team writes a design
          proposal with requirements, boards, a model, data, metrics, and
          budgets, and the instructor reviews it.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Learning outcomes}
  After this day you can:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Plan model versions and over-the-air updates for a group of devices.
    \item Identify security and privacy threats of an edge AI system and
          select mitigations.
    \item Estimate the power budget and the cost of a deployment.
    \item Write a system design proposal.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
