% Goal and plan of the Day 4 lab.
% The parts and the times come from the Day 4 section of the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} your group has a measured comparison of a
          \code{float32} model and an \code{int8} model: size, accuracy,
          and latency, on the laptop and on the XIAOML Kit.
    \item \textbf{Board:} the XIAOML Kit. Only Part D needs the kit.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} the file \code{report.md} with the
          comparison table of Part D and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the mapping, the two methods, and the search for the
    layer that loses accuracy. In the lab, you write the mapping, you
    quantize a model with the two methods, and you measure the float model
    and the \code{int8} model on the board.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{Each \code{int8} number needs the float number next to it,
    from the same test set and the same board.}
\end{frame}
