\begin{frame}{Decision Log}
  Write about 100 words. State one design decision, give your measured
  numbers, and name the trade-off.
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{exerciseblock}
    The motion classifier of Day 3 goes into a product with the XIAO
    ESP32S3. Which model do you put on the board?
    \begin{itemize}
      \item The \code{float32} model.
      \item The \code{int8} model.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{exerciseblock}
  \vskip 0.2em
  A good Decision Log:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item gives the flash, the arena, the latency, and the accuracy of the
          two models from your table,
    \item explains the change of the accuracy, or explains why the accuracy
          does not change. Use the clipped input values and your
          calibration set.
    \item names what you lose with your decision. Example: a smaller model
          against the work for the calibration set and the quantization
          code.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
