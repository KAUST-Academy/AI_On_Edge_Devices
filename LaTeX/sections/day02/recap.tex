% Recap of the day. Three to five points, then the one point to keep.
\begin{frame}{Recap}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item A microcontroller has flash for the program, RAM for the data,
          and buses for the sensors: I2C, SPI, I2S, and UART.
    \item The sampling rate must be more than two times the highest
          frequency of the signal. A lower rate gives aliasing.
    \item A window is the input of the model. Its length and its overlap
          are design decisions.
    \item Overlapping windows of one recording are almost equal. Split the
          data by recording session, not by window.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.5em
  \keypoint{Fix the sampling rate, the labels, and the split before you
            train. A model cannot repair an error in its data.}
\end{frame}
