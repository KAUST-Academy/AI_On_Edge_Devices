% Motivation and learning outcomes.
% The outcomes are the outcomes of Day 2 in the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Why this day matters}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item A model is only as good as its data. On an edge device, you make
          the data: the sensor, the sampling rate, and the labels are your
          decisions.
    \item To read a sensor, you must know the memory, the clock, and the
          buses of the microcontroller.
    \item In the lab you read the IMU of the XIAOML Kit with MicroPython
          and record a labelled motion dataset.
    \item Day 3 trains a model on this dataset. An error in the data today
          gives a wrong accuracy tomorrow.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Learning outcomes}
  After this day you can:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Describe the memory, the clock, and the buses of a
          microcontroller.
    \item Select a sampling rate and a window length for a sensor signal.
    \item Write a MicroPython program that reads a sensor and sends the
          data.
    \item Build a labelled dataset with a correct split between training
          and test data.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
