\begin{frame}{Decision Log}
  \begin{exerciseblock}
    A shop uses 20 Raspberry Pi cameras to count the people at its doors.
    One camera stands near a window, so its light changes with the time of
    day. The shop has one person who looks at alerts.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Which metrics does each device send, how often, and where are
            they stored?
      \item Which alert rules do you use, with which thresholds, windows,
            and durations?
      \item How many false alerts each week do you expect, and what does
            the person do for each alert?
    \end{itemize}
  \end{exerciseblock}
  \vskip 0.2em
  A good Decision Log (about 100 words):
  \begin{itemize}
    \item gives your measured size of a summary, your threshold, and the
          delay of your alert,
    \item explains the camera near the window: one reference is not enough,
    \item names what you lose: for example a fast alert against fewer false
          alerts.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
