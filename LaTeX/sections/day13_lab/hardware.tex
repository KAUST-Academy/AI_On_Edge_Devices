  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \code{monitor\_detect.py} runs the Day 8 detector. Each 10 s it
          writes one summary to the log and sends it to
          \code{edgeai/g07/pi/metrics}.
    \item \code{dashboard.py} stores each summary and draws eight charts.
          \code{alert.py} applies one rule and publishes its state on
          \code{edgeai/g07/pi/alert/<metric>}.

\begin{frame}{The summary of each 10 s}
  \footnotesize
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.12}
  \setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
  \begingroup\centering
  \begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.30\textwidth}L{0.62\textwidth}@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Field} & \textbf{Content} \\
    \midrule
    \texttt{frames}, \texttt{fps} & Frames in the window, frames each
      second \\
    \texttt{latency\_ms}, \texttt{latency\_p95\_ms}, \texttt{latency\_max\_ms}
      & Time of one frame: mean, 95th percentile, largest \\
    \texttt{confidence} & Mean top score: the largest box score of each
      frame, 0 with no box \\
    \texttt{low\_conf} & Part of the frames with a top score below 0.5 \\
    \texttt{objects} & Mean number of boxes with a score of 0.25 or more \\
    \texttt{brightness}, \texttt{sharpness} & Mean of the grey image,
      variance of its Laplacian (Task A1) \\
    \texttt{cpu\_temp\_c}, \texttt{throttled} & Temperature, the bits of
      \texttt{vcgencmd get\_throttled} \\
    \texttt{rss\_mb}, \texttt{ram\_used\_mb}, \texttt{disk\_free\_pct} &
      Memory of the detector, of the board; free disk \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}\par\endgroup
  \vskip 0.4em
  \small
  The model runs with the score threshold 0.05, so that the top score is
  known also when the model is not sure. The message also has the fields of
  HW-08: \code{seq}, \code{ts}, \code{cpu\_percent}.
\end{frame}
