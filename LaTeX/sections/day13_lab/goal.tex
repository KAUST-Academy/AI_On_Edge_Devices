% Goal and plan of the Day 13 lab.
% The parts and the times come from the Day 13 section of the syllabus.
\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} a monitored inference application. The Day 8
          detector measures itself, writes a log, and sends its metrics to a
          live dashboard. An alert rule tells you about drift.
    \item \textbf{Board:} the Raspberry Pi 5 with the camera.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} the live dashboard during a drift event,
          and the file \code{report.md} with the measured values, a short
          incident report, and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the metrics of a device and of a model, structured
    logs with rotation, thresholds from a history, and an alert rule with a
    window and a duration. Its values came from stored images and a
    simulation. Today the camera sees your scene, and you change the light
    yourself.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{Record a normal reference first. A threshold has a meaning only
    against your own normal values.}
\end{frame}
