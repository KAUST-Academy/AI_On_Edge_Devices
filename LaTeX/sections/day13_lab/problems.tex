    \code{the Day 8 file pi/detector.py is not found}
      & Copy the Day 8 folder to \code{\textasciitilde/edgeai/day08/} \\
    The Day 8 model is not on the card
      & Download \code{yolo11n.pt} once, then \code{--model yolo11n.pt
        --imgsz 320} \\
    The charts stay empty
      & Start the detector with \code{--group g07} and no
        \code{--no-mqtt} \\
    The state bar is yellow
      & No summary for 25 s: look at the terminal of the detector \\
    The alert fires in the normal scene
      & A longer reference, or \code{--min-margin 0.05} \\
    The alert does not fire during the event
      & An event longer than 2 minutes, or \code{--for-seconds 30} \\
    The light of the room cannot change
      & \code{--dim-after 60 --dim-gain 0.1 --dim-seconds 180} \\
    A task is not complete in time
      & Use the file of \code{solutions/pi/}, and write this in the
        report \\
