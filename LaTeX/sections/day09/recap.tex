% Recap of the day. Three to five points, then the one point to keep.
\begin{frame}{Recap}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item A benchmark looks at the system, at the model, and at the data. A
          standard suite such as MLPerf Tiny fixes the task, the dataset, and
          the run rules, so that two results compare.
    \item A fair measurement has a warm-up, many repetitions, and fixed
          inputs. Report the median and the 95th percentile of the latency,
          not only the mean.
    \item The time of the full pipeline is longer than the time of the model.
          A report says which of the two times it gives.
    \item The energy of one inference is the active power times the latency.
          The duty cycle gives the mean power, and the mean power gives the
          battery life. Record the temperature: a hot processor is slower.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.5em
  \keypoint{A number with no method is not a result. For each number, give the
            device, the model file, the input, the warm-up, the number of
            repetitions, and the statistic.}
\end{frame}
