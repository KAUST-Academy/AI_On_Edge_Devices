\begin{frame}{Goal of this lab}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Goal:} your group has one benchmark report for the XIAOML
          Kit and the Raspberry Pi 5.
    \item \textbf{Boards:} the XIAOML Kit and the Raspberry Pi 5.
    \item \textbf{Deliverable:} the file \code{report.md} with the protocol,
          the result tables, and the Decision Log.
  \end{itemize}
  \vskip 0.4em
  \begin{block}{From the lecture to the lab}
    The lecture gave the method: the seven parts of a benchmark, the
    warm-up, the timed runs, the percentiles, the window, the energy, and
    the temperature. All its measured values are from an x86 computer or
    from other boards. Today you measure your two boards.
  \end{block}
  \vskip 0.2em
  \keypoint{Each number of your report has its method: the warm-up runs,
    the timed runs, the input, the statistic, and the window.}
\end{frame}
